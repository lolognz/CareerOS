# Manfred platform research

## Purpose and provenance

This file preserves a conservative working understanding of Manfred's freelance service. It is channel research, not candidate evidence and not a description of any concrete freelance opportunity.

Provenance categories used below:

- `platform_statement`: a claim published by Manfred; attributed, not independently verified.
- `user_observation`: a fact observed by the user in the platform interface or relationship.
- `inference`: a bounded interpretation that still requires confirmation.
- `unknown`: information that available sources do not resolve.

## Current understanding

### Service and intermediary relationship

- **`platform_statement`:** Manfred presents its freelance service as a recruiting and accompaniment channel that surfaces curated projects, provides project context, and assists with evaluating fit, pricing, negotiation, and changes during a project.
- **`platform_statement`:** Manfred states that project information may include scope, expected duration, expectations, and price, and that it filters or clarifies opportunities before presenting them.
- **`inference`:** These statements support modeling Manfred as a persistent sourcing/intermediation channel rather than as the client or project itself.

### Published contractual and commercial model

- **`platform_statement`:** Manfred presents the relationship as `Company -> Manfred -> Freelancer`.
- **`platform_statement`:** The selected freelancer signs a framework agreement with Manfred and a project-specific appendix or addendum for each project.
- **`platform_statement`:** The final company has its commercial agreement with Manfred.
- **`platform_statement`:** The freelancer invoices Manfred at the agreed freelancer rate.
- **`platform_statement`:** Manfred invoices the final company for the freelancer rate plus its commercial fee.
- **`platform_statement`:** Manfred publicly describes that fee as 20% of the hourly rate in this freelance recruiting model.
- **`platform_statement`:** Manfred states that this structure allows it to advance freelancer invoice payments and mediate during the relationship.
- **`unknown`:** These are Manfred-published descriptions, not independent guarantees. The exact agreement and operating terms must be checked for every concrete project.

### Pricing guidance

- **`platform_statement`:** Manfred's guidance says freelance pricing should consider desired earnings or equivalent compensation, realistically billable time, specialization, and the risk assumed in a project.
- **`platform_statement`:** Manfred warns against directly converting an employee annual salary into freelance billing because freelance work does not automatically include fully billable time or paid employment conditions.
- **`platform_statement`:** Manfred discusses hourly, daily, per-project, and monthly or retainer pricing models, with the appropriate model depending on scope, variability, continuity, and risk.
- **`inference`:** This guidance is useful context but does not establish Manuel's required rate. Any calculation must use the separate [global freelance rate strategy](../../rate-strategy.md) and explicit current assumptions.

### Desired-salary field

- **`user_observation`:** The profile exposes a generic `salario deseado` field while the selected contract type is freelance.
- **`platform_statement`:** Manfred's general profile guidance describes the field in employment-salary terms and says it is used alongside stack and experience to adjust opportunities sent to a candidate.
- **`unknown`:** How Manfred interprets the generic `salario deseado` field for a freelance-only profile is not established by the reviewed sources.
- **`unknown`:** It is not known whether the value represents employee-equivalent salary, annual freelance billing, a matching signal, or another internal convention. No interpretation should be invented.

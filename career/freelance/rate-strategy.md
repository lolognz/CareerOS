# Freelance rate strategy

## Purpose and boundary

This file contains the global economic decision framework for evaluating freelance opportunities, regardless of sourcing channel. It is career strategy, not canonical candidate knowledge, tax advice, or a rate calculator.

No hourly, daily, monthly, or annual billing target is established yet. Those values require explicit assumptions and current tax inputs.

- **Current fixed gross salary:** EUR 51,000 per year.
- **Normal monthly net cash received:** approximately EUR 3,000.
- **Restaurant vouchers:** an additional component exists, but its exact amount is not yet recorded.
- **Annual bonus:** exists.
- **Most recent bonus:** EUR 5,023 gross, calculated from the previous annual salary of EUR 50,230.
- **Employment conditions:** current employment provides employee stability and paid employment conditions that freelance billing does not automatically reproduce.

An employee annual net total must not be calculated yet. The salary payment frequency and some benefit details remain unresolved.

## Freelance objective

The purpose of moving to freelance is a material economic improvement, not merely higher gross billing.

The current target is approximately EUR 4,000–5,000 of average monthly net disposable income after:

- applicable income taxes;
- self-employed Social Security contributions;
- normal professional operating expenses.

This is a conditional range, not a single fixed requirement and not yet a derived billing target.

### Conditions supporting the lower end

A project may justify a result closer to EUR 4,000 per month when it offers strong non-monetary value, such as:

- long expected duration;
- fully remote work;
- high stability or predictability;
- strong technical interest;
- strong career-development value;
- good flexibility and working conditions.

### Conditions requiring the upper end

A project should require a result closer to EUR 5,000 per month when it carries greater disadvantages or risk, such as:

- short or uncertain duration;
- lower career value;
- weaker technical interest;
- reduced flexibility or more required presence;
- higher project or client risk;
- worse overall conditions.

No precise scoring formula exists yet.

## Sustainable annual-average definition

The eventual net target must be evaluated as average sustainable monthly net income across the full year, not merely as net cash during a fully billed month.

Future calculations must account for:

- non-billable vacation;
- holidays;
- reasonable non-billable administrative and commercial time;
- possible gaps between projects;
- Social Security contributions;
- income tax;
- normal professional expenses.

VAT is not personal income and must never be counted as such.

## Calculations deliberately deferred

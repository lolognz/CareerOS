# Freelance

`career/freelance/` contains reusable career strategy and workflows for evaluating freelance work. It is not canonical candidate knowledge: professional facts and evidence remain under `knowledge/` and should be referenced rather than copied here.

## Boundary

A sourcing channel is a persistent source or intermediary that may surface multiple freelance opportunities over time. A channel is not:

- an employer;
- a job application;
- a freelance client or project;
- a canonical candidate entity.

Future concrete freelance opportunities must have independent dossiers that reference their sourcing channel. No opportunity dossier model is defined yet; it should emerge from the first real opportunity rather than from a speculative abstraction.

This area deliberately has no schema, lifecycle, generalized marketplace model, or generator.
